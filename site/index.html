共振 Resonance

用故事回應故事 Answer stories with stories

一個用故事卡片彼此交流的地方：回應不是按讚，而是另一段故事 A place to share life stories as cards, where a reply isn’t a like but a story of your own.

我們相信故事的力量：每個人生而立體而獨特，都帶著自己珍貴的人生經驗；而故事，是串聯人與人、發揮影響力最好的途徑，這是我們打造「共振」的原因。使用者把一段人生寫成一張故事卡片；讀到觸動自己的故事時，回應不是按讚，而是寫下自己的卡片去「共振」它，讓生命影響生命。 We believe in the power of stories. Every person is born whole, many-sided and unique, carrying life experience no one else has, and stories are the best way to connect people and make a difference. That is why we built Resonance. People write a stretch of their life as a story card. When a story moves them, they don’t answer with a like: they write a card of their own to resonate with it. Let lives influence lives.

  • 共振，不是按讚Resonance, not likes

    公開回應一張卡片的唯一方式，是寫另一張卡片。沒有讚數，也沒有公開留言區The one public way to answer a card is to write another card. No like counts, no public comment threads.

  • 對話從故事開始Conversations start from a story

    沒有開放的收件匣。每段對話都從一個故事開始，而不是「嗨，在嗎」There is no open inbox. Every conversation starts from a story, never from “hi”.

  • AI 是鏡子，不是裁判AI as a mirror, not a judge

    發布前，共振會告訴你它在草稿裡讀到的核心體悟。沒有廣告，不追蹤你Before you publish, Resonance shows you the core insight it read in your draft. No ads, no tracking.
